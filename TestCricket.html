What is Test Cricket ?

Test cricket is the longest form of the sport of cricket. Test matches are played between national representative teams that have been granted Test status, by the International Cricket Council (ICC). The two teams of 11 players each play a four-innings match, which may last up to five days. Test cricket is considered the game's highest standard, although limited overs cricket has become more popular for casual fans since the 1990s. Test matches are played in traditional white kit and using red cricket balls, unlike the other forms of cricket which feature coloured kits and white balls. Test cricket is usually played in days with six hours of play, divided into three two-hour sessions. Breaks inlcude luunch and tea breaks. At the end of the day the umpires will call stumps and the game will resume again the next day. The team that scores the most runs wins the match. If the match is not completed within five days, it is declared a draw. There are no limits on the number of overs in a Test match, and the team batting last must either score the required runs to win or be dismissed (all out) to lose. There are two tournaments that are played in Test Cricket called: The Ashes and the ICC World Test Championship. The Ashes is a series of Test matches played between England and Australia. The ICC World Test Championship is a league competition for Test cricket run by the International Cricket Council (ICC), which began in 2019. The current champions of the Ashes are Australia. The current champions of the ICC World Test Championship are South Africa. The next ICC World Test Championshipis underway between 2025-2027.

Test Cricket Record Holders:

  • Most Runs in Test Cricket: Sachin Tendulkar (India) - 15,921 runs
  • Most Wickets in Test Cricket: Muttiah Muralitharan (Sri Lanka) - 800 wickets
  • Highest Individual Score in Test Cricket: Brian Lara (West Indies) - 400 not out
  • Most Centuries in Test Cricket: Sachin Tendulkar (India) - 51 centuries
  • Most Matches Played in Test Cricket: Sachin Tendulkar (India) - 200 matches
